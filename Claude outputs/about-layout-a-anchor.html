FILE 01 About me

Sixteen years of signal.
Now, software.

I'm Lyn Smith. I spent the last sixteen years in the Marine Corps leading technical teams — analyzing radio signals, turning messy data into clear answers, and teaching people to do both. Along the way I noticed the part of the work I loved most: building the tools.

So that's what I do now. I'm studying full-stack development at San Diego Global Knowledge University, working in Python, Django, JavaScript, and SQL — this site is one of the projects. The full story, resume-style, is on the experience page.
