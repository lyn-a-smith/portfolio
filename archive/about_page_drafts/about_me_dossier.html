FILE 01 Mission brief

Sixteen years of signal.
Now, software.

For sixteen years, my job has been finding the signal in the noise — pulling meaning out of the electromagnetic spectrum, turning massive, messy datasets into intelligence that leaders could actually act on. I've led SIGINT crews, planned electromagnetic warfare operations, built analytic tools with Python, and briefed everyone from junior Marines to generals.

Along the way I noticed the work I loved most: building the thing. The script that automated a day of analyst grind. The scripting environment that made training feel real. The workflow that turned a 12-hour slide-polishing ritual into a live dashboard.

So I'm re-tasking. I'm now a full-stack development student at San Diego Global Knowledge University, stacking Python, Django, JavaScript and SQL on top of a career spent making complex systems make sense. Same mission, new terrain: build tools that cut through noise.

Lyn Smith
Chief Warrant Officer, USMC · Yuma, Arizona
