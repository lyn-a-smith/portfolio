The plot twist

My favorite moments in uniform were never the briefings — they were the builds. The Python script that gave analysts their day back. The scripting environment that made a training exercise feel like the real thing. The dashboard that replaced a 12-hour slide-formatting ritual.

Sixteen years in, I realized the pattern: I don't just want to analyze the system. I want to build it.

So while serving as a warrant officer at the Marine Corps' premier aviation training squadron, I enrolled in San Diego Global Knowledge University's Full-Stack Development Immersive. Different terrain, same mission: take something complex, make it useful, ship it.
